Adding EOG to the sleep-staging student

The 175,599-parameter EEG + EOG student reaches 81.96% accuracy and κ 0.756 on the held-out test set. That beats the shipped N4kd and the 412K-parameter teacher it learned from. This page covers what got there, what didn't help, and what to do next.

Architecture and parameter count

N4kd's backbone is kept unchanged. Three branches are added, each through a gate that starts closed (zero projection, gate bias −4), so training begins from exactly N4kd's function. During training, modality dropout switches EOG and the TF branch off for a quarter of the sequences. As a result, the model still works when a channel is missing.

Training (Stage 3). Warm start from N4kd, with the backbone frozen for 10 epochs and then trained at 0.1× learning rate. Knowledge distillation from ENSmc (α 0.5, T 3), on corrected labels. Boundary-aware label smoothing (ε 0.2) and an auxiliary transition loss (0.1). Flat or stuck epochs are left out of the loss. Gain augmentation of 0.75–1.33×, EMA weights, and AdamW at 3e-4 for 75 epochs, with sequences of 256 epochs. Three seeds, which agree within 0.001 κ on validation.

How it got there

Label-alignment bug found and fixed biggest early gain

The preprocessing dropped unscored epochs from the labels before computing the trim, then cut the full signal. This shifted the labels on 54/137 train, 13/31 validation and 13/29 test recordings, and 5–7% of all labels were wrong. Re-scoring the same N4kd against corrected labels: validation 76.1% → 79.2%, test 77.7% → 79.0%.

Stage A: EOG alone, 5-fold CV formally not shown

κ +0.011, but the CI was [−0.004, +0.026], so it failed the pre-set bar. Paired person by person (85 subjects) the gain was clear: +0.012 κ, CI [+0.004, +0.020], p = 0.004. EOG was kept.

Stage 1: advanced arms, 5-fold CV, corrected labels full HELPS

full vs base: κ +0.018 [+0.0095, +0.026], better in all 5 folds and for 65/85 subjects. full_eeg (no EOG) gained only +0.007, below the bar. EOG's own share is +0.011 (p = 6e-5).

Stage 3: production training, 3 seeds bar passed

Validation κ 0.742–0.743 against a pre-set bar of 0.732. N1 F1 0.560–0.566 (bar 0.538), REM F1 0.834–0.840 (bar 0.802). Seed 1 was chosen on validation macro-F1.

One test evaluation 81.96%, κ 0.756

What worked and what didn't

Worked

  • Correcting the labelsN4kd +3.1 pts on validation and +1.3 on test, from scoring alone.
  • EOG as its own gated branch+0.011 κ over the EEG-only arm in CV. On test, removing it costs 0.012 κ, 2.3 pts of REM and 2.0 of N1.
  • Time-frequency (sigma band) branchRemoving it costs 0.014 κ on test, mostly N2 and REM.
  • Closed gates, warm start, modality dropoutSeeds agree within 0.001 κ, and the model degrades gracefully without EOG.
  • Distillation, then beating the teacherκ 0.756 vs ENSmc 0.732, at 43% of its size.
  • ST (telemetry) nightsκ 0.678 → 0.746 and N1 48.8 → 59.5, helped by gain augmentation and the quality mask.
  • Calibration for freeA fitted temperature of 1.002 means no correction is needed.
  • Preloading data on Kaggle12 ms → 0.4 ms per window, so Stage 1 fitted in one session.
  • The frozen verifierIt blocked every bad report in every run.

Didn't work, or didn't help

  • Five fold means as the only testToo coarse for a ~0.01 κ effect. Person-level pairing was needed.
  • TF + local + recipe without EOG+0.007 κ in CV, below the bar.
  • Sequence decodersThe REM min-run decoder adds 0.0000 κ on the new model, and HMM added ≤ 0.002 on N4kd.
  • N1Still the weakest stage at F1 50.7, mostly confused with N2 and W.
  • N3 in cross-validation1–2 points lower in CV (not on test, +1.0). Keep watching it.
  • First theory of the bad ST nights"Hypnogram-relative onsets" was wrong. The cause was the drop-then-trim bug.
  • Run-time estimatesThe larger arms took 1.6–2.2× longer than estimated. The early runs were bound by data loading.
  • llama-server as the report runtimeIt scored 20–23/31 on dev vs 30/31 for the evaluated llama-completion path. 8 of 31 outputs differ.
  • Report model on new test packets27/29 instead of 29/29. It stays safe (one blocked), but is less complete.
  • Attribution (Gate 3a)Not recomputed for the new model, so the packet block is empty.

Roadmap

Now · 1–2 weeks

Make it shippable

  1. Retrain the report model on the new packets. The 123 + 14 examples are built; target dev ≥ 30/31, then one test run.
  2. Move deployment to the evaluated llama-completion path, or find what differs in llama-server.
  3. Freeze frozen_v2.json with the new model, tables and report-model hashes.
  4. Optional: recompute attribution for the report footer.
Next · 1–2 months

Improve, with fresh data

  1. Validate externally on a new dataset. The CFS tensors in the repo are whole-night summaries, so CFS needs a fresh download and preprocessing. No more tuning on this test set.
  2. Test the second EEG lead (Pz-Oz, already in the tensors), then chin EMG (a 1 Hz envelope in SC, raw in ST, so it needs harmonising). Both fit the 200K budget.
  3. Train longer: the best epochs were 68–72 of 75.
  4. Average or distil the three Stage 3 seeds.
  5. Rebuild future datasets with the fixed preprocessing.
Later

Edge device

  1. Raspberry Pi 5: route EEG + EOG to Stage 3, and EEG-only to Stage 3 with EOG off.
  2. Export the staging model to ONNX; it takes seconds per night on a CPU.
  3. Agentic layer for QC, study-period detection, routing, fallback and audit around the frozen pipeline.
The test set has now been read for this model line, once for staging and once through the report pipeline, and both reads are logged. Using it to choose between further variants would make it a selection set, so new ideas should be judged on cross-validation and an external dataset.
