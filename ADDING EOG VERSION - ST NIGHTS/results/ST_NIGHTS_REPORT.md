## A7. Are the expert labels shifted in time? (offset scan)

Every large offset is NEGATIVE (labels ahead of the signal), and almost all are ST recordings. `distillation/preprocess_multichannel.py` (copied unchanged from `code/Phase1_48/Phase1reworked/preprocess.py`) builds labels from `mne.read_annotations(hyp_path).onset`, whose onsets count from the HYPNOGRAM file's start, not from `raw.annotations` (re-aligned to the PSG start by `set_annotations`). If a hypnogram starts Δ later than its PSG, every label lands Δ early - exactly this signature. Confirm with `verify_hypnogram_alignment.py` on a machine that has the raw Sleep-EDF files.
