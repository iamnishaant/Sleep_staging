Model size

Student vs teacher. Our 175,599-parameter student is better than its 412,311-parameter teacher on every test metric: 81.96% vs 80.02% accuracy, κ 0.756 vs 0.732. That's 43% of the teacher's size. The usual distillation result is a student that comes close to its teacher; the EOG branch and training recipe take this one past it.
The whole system isn't small. The staging model is 0.7 MB. The report model (Llama-3.2-3B-Student, Q4_K_M) is 2.0 GB on disk and peaks at about 4.7 GB of RAM on x86. On an edge device, the report model is the cost.

Is there novelty?

This assessment comes from targeted literature searches on 14 October 2026, not a systematic review. Any paper claim should be phrased "to our knowledge".

Established, so not claimed

  • Lightweight CNN + Transformer stagingMany published models, some smaller than ours.
  • EEG + EOG fusionUsed by LightSleepX, SalientSleepNet, LMCSleepNet and others.
  • Knowledge distillation and multi-scale front endsStandard techniques.
  • Grammar-constrained decoding and local LLMsGeneral tooling.

Plausibly new

  • Verified, evidence-cited sleep reports from a small local LLMEvery claim cites a packet field and must pass a frozen verifier before release. Shipped test: 29/29, 0 violations, fidelity 1.000. The same base model without this training scores 2/31.
  • Uncertainty carried into the report and enforcedUnreliable values must be hedged, and reliable ones must not be. The verifier checks both.
  • A sub-200K student that beats its 412K teacherGated EEG + EOG fusion that starts from the distilled function and still works without EOG.
  • A label-alignment findingThe drop-then-trim preprocessing shifted 5–7% of labels. Fixing it alone is worth +1.3 to +3 points.
  • Proposed: an offline, auditable agent on a single-board computerEdge staging exists; we found no end-to-end staging and verified reporting on a Pi-class device. It isn't built yet.
To make the claim stronger: run the standard Sleep-EDF-78 protocol once; evaluate on an external dataset; measure on a real Pi 5; and report violation rates of the same LLM with and without the grammar and verifier.
